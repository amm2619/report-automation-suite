(U) Topic A

Views are the primary axis (what users actually switch between most). Project lives as a compact segmented control on the right of the same row.

(U) Topic B

Switching view preserves project selection, and vice versa — matches how people actually compare one tab across projects.

(U) Topic C

Total chrome drops from ~75px to ~38px. On a 900px laptop that's ~4% of the viewport back.

Mechanics

  • State: (project, view) pair stored together.
  • Switch view → project stays.
  • Switch project → view stays.
  • Fits Sprint/PMR too — any report with 2 tab axes.

What to change

  • New helper buildDualTabBar({views, projects, ...}).
  • runEOS: emit one .optB-bar per frame instead of nested .r-tabs + .r-subtabs.
  • switchRtab/switchRstab merge into one resolver keyed on data-axis.
  • Sprint report (Plan/Report × 6 subtabs) is the other big win.

Tradeoffs

  • Segment scales to ~5 projects. Beyond that, swap to a dropdown.
  • Sprint has 6 inner views — still fits but tight on narrow windows; overflow scrolls.
  • Risk: touches Weekly/EOS/PMR/Sprint render paths. Medium blast radius; keep as single refactor commit.
